THE RULES

How to play

Easy to learn. Surprisingly tactical.

  1. Players take turns drawing one horizontal or vertical line between neighboring dots.
  2. Finish the fourth side of a box to claim it. Your color and initials appear inside.
  3. Claiming one or more boxes earns an extra turn.
  4. When every box is claimed, the player with the most boxes wins. Ties are possible.
  5. Play against 1–16 CPUs, 2–20 people on one device, or an online room with multiple friends.

Online rooms use WebRTC peer-to-peer connections and the public PeerJS signaling service. All players need internet access; the host must keep their tab open. Some restrictive networks may block peer-to-peer connections.
