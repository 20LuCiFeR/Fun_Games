♠ Bluff ♦

Play 2 or more cards face-down, claim a rank, and bluff if you dare. First to empty their hand wins.
Play with friends onlinePeer-to-peer: nothing to install, works from this page. Everyone opens the same link.
Rules: play at least 2 cards each turn. If you have 1 card, you may Pass or call Bluff!. Only the next player may challenge. Disconnected players have 20 seconds to return. Their remaining hand is discarded after timeout.
